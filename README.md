# 🧠 Master Knowledge Vault & Research Notes

> **Core Philosophy**: *The Expandable Domain-Stack (Dewey-Zettelkasten Hybrid)*
> 
> As a Machine Learning Engineer aspirant, your knowledge naturally evolves across software engineering, mathematical foundations, data science tools, advanced AI architectures (ML $\rightarrow$ DL $\rightarrow$ CV $\rightarrow$ NLP $\rightarrow$ RL $\rightarrow$ Robotics), as well as your creative & scientific curiosities (Physics, Chemistry, Poetry, Biographies like Van Gogh).
> 
> This repository uses a **Numbered Domain Taxonomy (00 - 90)** designed to scale indefinitely without cluttering your workspace or requiring structural overhauls.

## 🧭 How to Manage & Grow Your Notes

1. **Numeric Sorting in VS Code**:
   - Folders are numbered (`00_`, `10_`, `20_`, `30_`, `40_`, `50_`, `80_`, `90_`) so they always stay in chronological and logical order in your editor sidebar.

2. **Progression-Friendly Structure**:
   - **Current Focus**: Start with `10_CS_&_Engineering/11_Languages/Python`, `30_Data_Science_&_Tools/31_NumPy`, `40_AI_&_Machine_Learning/41_Machine_Learning`.
   - **Future Escalation**: As you progress to Deep Learning, Computer Vision, NLP, RL, and Robotics, drop your Jupyter Notebooks (`.ipynb`) or Markdown files directly into `40_AI_&_Machine_Learning/42_Deep_Learning`, `43_Computer_Vision`, `44_NLP`, `46_Reinforcement_Learning`, and `50_Robotics_&_Automation`.

3. **Interdisciplinary Space**:
   - Keep your creative and curiosity notes (Poetry, Van Gogh, Quantum Physics, Chemistry breakthroughs) clean and organized inside `80_Humanities_&_Curiosity/`.

---

## 📌 Rules of Note Taking
- **Atomic Notebooks**: Keep Jupyter notebooks (`.ipynb`) focused on single concepts (e.g. `01_Array_creation.ipynb`, `02_Matrix_multiplication.ipynb`).
- **Markdown Headers**: Use clear H1 `#` and H2 `##` headers in every markdown note.
- **Code + Math**: Combine Python code blocks with LaTeX LaTeX equations (`\( y = wx + b \)`) for deep learning formulas.
